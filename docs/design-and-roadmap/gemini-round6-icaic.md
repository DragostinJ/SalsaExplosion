# Image brief: Salsa Explosion website, round 6 (ICAIC rollout)

## Instructions for Gemini

Create **one separate image for every numbered item below**: 12 images in total, in this order.

- Every image is square 1:1.
- **No text, letters, numbers, logos, signage or watermarks inside any image.** Clock and stopwatch faces are blank or marked with small stars.
- Under each image in your reply, write its number and title (e.g. "1. Arch of hands") so I can tell them apart.
- If you can't make all 12 in one reply, make **Part A** first, then continue with **Part B** when I say "continue".

All 12 images are **one matching set** of flat screen-print illustrations in the style of 1960s–70s Cuban ICAIC film posters. They must look like they came from the same print studio as each other, and as the earlier rounds (maracas, map and pin, tear-off calendar, five stars, shopping bag, sealed letter, door key, dance footprints, clave sticks).

**Shared style for all 12:** flat three-colour screenprint illustration in the style of 1960s–70s Cuban ICAIC film posters: bold simple cut-out shapes, ink black, vermilion red and mustard yellow only, visible paper grain, slight ink misregistration between the colours, halftone dots in the shadows. **On a perfectly plain, flat warm cream background, with nothing touching the edges and no shadow under the object.** No frame. The object centred with a generous empty margin. People are bold silhouettes with no facial features.

## Part A: the eight move families (8 images)

### 1. Arch of hands
A leader's and a follower's forearms reaching up and holding hands to make an arch, with a curved arrow sweeping under the arch to show a turn.

### 2. The wheel
A ring of eight small dancer silhouettes holding hands in a circle, seen from slightly above, with one larger raised hand in the middle of the ring, as if calling the next move.

### 3. Dancing shoes
A pair of two-tone dance shoes, one flat on the ground and one up on its toe as if mid-step, with short motion lines and three small stars around them.

### 4. Body wave
A dancer's torso in side profile as a bold silhouette, from the neck to the hips, with three curved ripple lines across the chest, the ribs and the hips showing a rolling body wave.

### 5. Conga and handkerchief
A tall conga drum with a knotted handkerchief draped over its rim, a few short strike marks above the drum head.

### 6. Tres and bongos
A small Cuban tres guitar (a little guitar with three pairs of strings) leaning against a pair of bongo drums tied together.

### 7. Street speaker
A big portable speaker sitting on a doorstep, round sound-wave rings coming out of it, a pair of trainers beside it.

### 8. Three batá drums
Three hourglass-shaped batá drums of three sizes, small, medium and large, lying side by side on their sides, each with lacing running along its body.

## Part B: the teachers' pages and the error page (4 images)

### 9. Clipboard
A clipboard holding a blank sheet with a column of tick boxes, three of them ticked, and a pencil lying across the corner.

### 10. Stopwatch and sound wave
An old stopwatch with a blank face marked only with small stars, a ribbon-like sound wave curling around it.

### 11. Film strip and magnifying glass
A curling strip of film, with a big magnifying glass held over one frame. Inside the lens, a single five-pointed star.

### 12. Dizzy drum
A conga drum knocked over on its side, with a few small stars and spirals circling above it like a dizzy cartoon.
