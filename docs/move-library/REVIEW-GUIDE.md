# Salsa Explosion UK: Maestro Enrique's Move Review Guide

Welcome to the **Faculty Move Review Desk**!  
Every move in the library is public from the start (owner decision, 2 November 2026). As a teacher you check the text, test the counts against real Cuban music, attach a clear video, and approve it: the move page then says "Checked on the music by" you. If a move is wrong or you don't teach it, mark it **Reject** (or **Needs Work**) and it disappears from the dancers' library.

---

## 5-Minute Quick Walkthrough (On Your Phone or Laptop)

### 1. Open the Review Desk
- On your phone or laptop, go to: **`/instructor/moves`**
- You will see the full list of 30 figures with status tags (*Draft*, *Approved*, *Needs Work*).

### 2. Pick a Move to Review
- Tap any move (for example, **Guapea** or **Dile Que No**).
- On the left (or top on mobile), you'll see how students will read the move: summary, leader footwork, and follower footwork.

### 3. Check It Against the Music (The Secret Weapon)
- Look at the section called **"Practise It in These Songs"**.
- Tap any song link (e.g., *Me Dicen Cuba — Tema 1*).
- It will open the real track in a new tab right at that moment in the song.
- **Dance or tap along**: Does the move fit the 8-count? Is it 1 eight-count, 2 eight-counts, or more?

### 4. Fine-Tune the Text (If Needed)
- Adjust the **Number of 8-Counts** if it takes longer than 1 count.
- Fix or simplify the **Leader Steps** or **Follower Steps** so dancers understand where to step and pause on 4 and 8.

### 5. Attach a Good Demonstration Video
- Tap **"Search YouTube"** to quickly find a clean Cuban Casino breakdown, or paste a YouTube video link.
- Enter the **Start** and **End** moments (like `0:30` to `1:15`) so students only see the move, not talking or advertisements.
- Tap **"Verify & Attach Video"**. A preview player will appear so you can confirm it looks clean and plays properly.

### 6. Verify and Approve
- Tick the mandatory box:  
  ☑️ **"I counted this on the music: the counts are right"**  
  *(The Approve button stays locked until you tick this!)*
- Select **Approve** (or *Needs Work* / *Reject* if the figure needs redesign).
- Tap **Save Review**.

### 7. Export Your Work
- Tap **← Back to Move Library**.
- At the top, you'll see a badge: **"1 Pending In Browser"** (or however many you reviewed).
- Tap **"Copy Review Report"**.
- Paste the text into WhatsApp or Slack to the developer (or Claude) so it gets permanently committed into the app!
