## Part A: photographs (6 images)

**Shared style for items 1, 5 and 6:** photorealistic editorial dance photograph, full-frame camera, natural film grain like Kodak Portra 800 pushed one stop. Deep near-black shadows, warm ivory highlights, colour accents ONLY in deep crimson red and warm amber gold. Real skin texture, real fabric weave, natural hands with exactly five fingers, no plastic or CGI look, no glossy retouching. Tasteful, athletic, joyful, documentary feel.

**Shared style for items 2, 3 and 4 ("stained-glass light"):** photorealistic editorial photograph, soft natural film grain. Warm late-afternoon Havana light coming through a stained-glass mediopunto (the semicircular fan window above Havana doorways), so the only strong colours are the glass colours (ruby red, amber, cobalt blue and emerald) falling as soft coloured patches. Peeling plaster, worn wood, real skin texture, natural hands with exactly five fingers, no CGI look.

Potted plants, a wrought-iron rail, high plaster walls. 35 mm, eye level, the couple centred.
